# Hardware Issues

## Description

Use this procedure when a user reports a problem with a physical computer or peripheral device.

Common hardware issues include keyboards, mice, monitors, docking stations, and power problems.

## Symptoms

* Keyboard or mouse does not respond.
* Monitor does not display an image.
* Laptop does not power on.
* External device is not detected.
* Docking station is not functioning.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm that the device is properly connected.
2. If the device is wireless, verify that it has sufficient battery power.
3. Disconnect and reconnect the device.
4. If appropriate, restart the computer.
5. Test the device with another compatible port or computer when available.
6. Determine whether the problem affects only one device or multiple devices.

## Resolution

For a disconnected or unresponsive peripheral, reconnect the device and verify that the computer recognizes it.

If the device continues to malfunction after basic troubleshooting, the hardware may require replacement or further technical inspection.

## Ticket Category

Hardware

## Escalation

Escalate the issue to the Hardware Support team if:

* The device appears physically damaged.
* The device does not work after basic troubleshooting.
* A replacement device may be required.
* The computer does not power on after checking the power connection.
